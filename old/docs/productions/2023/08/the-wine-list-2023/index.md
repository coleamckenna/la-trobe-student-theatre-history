## Abstract
"Some wines must be taken in context. It's not the wine itself, but its location."

Set over the course of a single night, The Wine List follows Susan Piker (Audrey Page) and Alex Treble (Amaryll Nesbit) as they delve into their troubled high-school partnership. Working through each year bottle by bottle, it is a show about love, accountability and the arduous process of trying to find what we want from our relationships. The Wine List is an entirely original work written and produced at STF.
