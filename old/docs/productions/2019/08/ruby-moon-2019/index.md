## Abstract
In picture-perfect Flaming Tree Grove, a little girl called Ruby sets off to visit her grandma at the end of the cul-de-sac. But she never arrives. Her parents spiral into suspicion and sorrow as they search for answers to the haunting mystery. Matt Cameron's fractured fairytale from the dark heart of suburbia.
