## Abstract
Two very different groups of students from neighbouring countries peer into the cultural chasm that divides them. Based on the ancient Sundanese tale of Jamarun — a poor man executed whose corpse is said to take on a beautiful fragrance, drawing birds and butterflies that blot out the sun. Presented through theatre, dance, and multimedia, in multiple languages.

Follows a 1997 co-production with IKIP Bandung (UPI) of Arifin C. Noer's The Bottomless Well.
