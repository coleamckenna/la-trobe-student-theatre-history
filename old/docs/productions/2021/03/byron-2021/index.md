## Abstract
Tony loves Byron. Byron loves Tony.

But there's a catch. Byron is a talking ferret.

Ash and Greta have accepted every wacky partner that their little brother has brought into their lives. But is this a step too far?

A play about love, family, and drawing lines in the sand. Byron delves into a strange new frontier of love and relationships that we'd probably rather not think about.
