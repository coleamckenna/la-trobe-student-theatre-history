## Abstract
You. Me. Butterflies. Adoration. Closing blinds. Hands.

In the midst of lockdown, a group of writers from STF formed the Six Lines Project — a weekly writing group that wrote six lines of dialogue in response to a prompt. By the end of 2020, the group had written well over 120 scripts. The Six Lines Showcase stages a small handful of these scripts, with four directors working with a team of actors to bring them to life.
