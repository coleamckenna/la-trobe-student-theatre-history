## Date
04/03/2020 – 13/03/2020

## Year
2020

## Venue

## Location
Carlton and Bundoora VIC, Australia

## Abstract
"A sad tale's best for winter…"

Two patriarchs from two royal families, great friends filled with admiration for each other, fall prey to a grave misunderstanding. This leads to an abandoned child, the death of a wrongly accused queen and two decades of guilt and resentment. However, absolution is never too late for those who seek it sincerely!

A cross-cultural collaboration between students from Hong Kong and Melbourne. The first part is set in 1920s colonial Hong Kong; the second in beachside 1970s Australia — a setting chosen to suit the play's movement between two distinct countries and to reflect the cultural diversity of the collaboration.
