# Mermaid

## Date

10/10/2018 – 13/10/2018

## Year

2018

## Author

Polly Teale

## Produced By

La Trobe University — The Myth, the Mystery, the Mayhem (2018 Three Pro Retold)

## Venue

Menzies College, La Trobe University

## Location

Bundoora VIC, Australia

## Abstract

Student-run production of Polly Teale's contemporary adaptation of Hans Christian Andersen's The Little Mermaid — not the Disney version. The work focuses on 21st-century themes of loss of voice and issues affecting teenagers.

Part of third-year Bachelor of Creative Arts drama students' end-of-semester performance projects, presented alongside The Pyjama Girl and One Man Two Guvnors.

## Categories
