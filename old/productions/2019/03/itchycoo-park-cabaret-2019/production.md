## Abstract

Let's take you back in time to ITCHYCOO PARK for La Trobe Student Theatre's 2019 Cabaret Show: "What did they do there? — They got high. What did they feel there? — Well they cried. But why the tears there? — They'll tell you why. It's all too beautiful…" Featuring songs from the psychedelic '60s, this year's cabaret brings together singers and performers from La Trobe Student Theatre.
