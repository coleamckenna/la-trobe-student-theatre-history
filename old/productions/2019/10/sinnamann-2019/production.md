## Abstract
A dramatic drag cabaret written, directed and performed by Tom Cattle for his final university performance. SINNAMANN discusses the obsessions of fame, success and achievement that can arise from pain and torment, and poses consequences for the super ego, which often steps in to protect a damaged soul.

Tom Cattle began drag at 20 as a form of rebellion. Dressing as a woman, he discovered a part of himself he had hidden — able to let go of the barriers and boxes perpetuated as the male 'Tom', transforming into a character that embodies everything from the world.
