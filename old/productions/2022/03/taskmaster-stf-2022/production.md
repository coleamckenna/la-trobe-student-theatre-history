# Taskmaster STF

## Date
18/03/2022

## Year
2022

## Author
Based on the Channel 4 series Taskmaster, created by Alex Horne

## Produced By
Student Theatre Foundation — Moat Fest 2022

## Venue
Menzies Theatre, Menzies College DH Building

## Abstract
For one night only, five STF personalities, old and new, compete in a series of ridiculous and hilarious tasks, testing their creativity and sometimes their patience. Their dignity is on the line — and it's all for the viewing pleasure of you, the audience. Lakshmi Ganapathy delivers a Student Theatre spin on Alex Horne's internationally beloved panel game show, in a riotously funny Moat launch not to be missed.

Moat Fest launch event: after the show, hang around for the Moat Festival 2022 launch (location to be announced).

## Director
Lakshmi Ganapathy

## Cast
Hosts: Fergus Black, Lakshmi Ganapathy. Contestants: Brigid Charis, Liam Jones, Alan Lam, Nathan Milne, Amy Parker
