## Abstract
A party in the 1920s is our window into the lives of New Yorkers and newlyweds Roland and Claire. The play delves into a world of decadence and ruptured mundanity, exploring strained relationships and the pain that comes with having to hide who you are. The production explored LGBTQ+ rights in the 1920s; Rolly, a married and closeted homosexual man, finds acceptance among his gay friends.

(Synopsis quoted from the production's Facebook event, cited in Stage Whispers.)
