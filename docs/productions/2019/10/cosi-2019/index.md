## Date
17/10/2019 – 19/10/2019

## Year
2019

## Author
Louis Nowra

## Produced By
La Trobe University — FESTIVAL 2019 (Creative Arts Festival)

## Venue
Menzies Building, La Trobe University

## Location
Bundoora VIC, Australia

## Abstract
Student-led performance project as part of the La Trobe Creative Arts Festival. Lewis arrives to direct a play with inmates at a mental institution, persuaded by Roy to stage Mozart's Cosi Fan Tutte — in 1971 Melbourne, against the background of the Vietnam War.

## Director
Jessica Polites, Wiaan V Venter

## Designer
Set: Madeline Atwell. Costume: Brigid Charis. Sound: Kye Richardson. Lighting: Simon Rogers.

## Cast
Jessica Polites (Cherry), Wiaan V Venter (Roy), Simon Rogers (Lewis), Madeline Atwell (Justine, Ruth), Brigid Charis (Zac), Kye Richardson (Doug), Troy Luke (Henry), Mikayla Kelly (Julie, Lucy), Max Draper (Nick)

## Crew
Production and stage management: Mikayla Kelly. Props: Troy Luke. Promotion, graphic design and photography: Max Draper. Sound and lighting operator: Jacob Hart.

## Notes
Thu 17 Oct 7pm; Fri 18 Oct 1pm; Sat 19 Oct 7pm. Part of graduating Bachelor of Creative Arts theatre students' final performance projects.

FESTIVAL, Student project
