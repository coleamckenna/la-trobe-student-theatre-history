## Abstract

You're on the 86. It's late. You're on the 86 to not be late. It's a CATCH-86. Come join Bush Poet Bill, Pauline Hanson and her (Uluru) rock collection, the Backbench Boys Bill Shorten and Barnaby Joyce, and Hac-Man, your favourite stupol hack, on a journey through La Trobe University via the 86. LTU Revue is a completely original comedy composed of skits and parody songs about La Trobe student life, politics, pop culture, and much more.
