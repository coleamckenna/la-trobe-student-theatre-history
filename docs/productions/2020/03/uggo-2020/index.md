## Abstract
Tired of living in a shallow world that rejects diversity, uni student Uggo plots to dismantle the patriarchy with words. The only things standing in the way of a feminist revolution are Uggo's cute housemate, an impromptu visit from her beauty-influencer sister, and the small matter of actually leaving her flat…

Uggo is a story about self-acceptance, deciding what defines you, and what happens when fear makes all your choices in life.
