## Abstract

Brighton, 1963. Francis Henshall, a failed skiffle player, has been fired and quickly finds himself employed by two very different masters. Master one: Rachel, disguised as her dead brother Roscoe Crabbe. Master two: Stanley, a posh twit who is both Rachel's lover and her brother's killer. Francis must keep his two guvnors apart — but that is easier said than done.

Richard Bean's adaptation of Carlo Goldoni's A Servant of Two Masters. Part of third-year Bachelor of Creative Arts drama students' end-of-semester performance projects.
