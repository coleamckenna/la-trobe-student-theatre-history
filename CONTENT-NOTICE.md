# Content notice

This repository contains **catalogue metadata** about La Trobe University Student
Theatre (LTUST) productions, exported for research and reference. It does **not**
redistribute archival materials.

## What is included

- Structured text fields (title, dates, cast, crew, venue, abstracts, etc.) derived
  From past students and web sources, source urls linked when available

## What is not included

- Programmes, photographs, PDFs, or other scanned documents

Where `source_url` appears in a production file, it points to the web source. Rights in underlying works (scripts, music, images, programmes) remain with
their respective creators, not with the
MIT license that covers this repository's **structure and scripts**.
