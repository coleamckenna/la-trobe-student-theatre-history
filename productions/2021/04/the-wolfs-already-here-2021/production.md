# The Wolf's Already Here

## Date

13/04/2021 – 17/04/2021

## Year

2021

## Author

Devised by Cole McKenna and Isabelle Piccolo-Cody

## Produced By

Student Theatre Foundation — Moat 2021

## Venue

Moat Theatre, La Trobe University

## Location

Bundoora VIC, Australia (site-specific, roving around campus)

## Abstract

I still love you, I don't know what to do.

When do you think you'll love me? Can you text me when you know. Love as possession, the beauty of existence, a threatening beast. How much do you love me? Can you write it on the back of my hand so I don't forget. Love past the end. Love as an always because there is no end.

A site-specific journey around the campus. A theatrical exploration of experiences and impressions of love. Devised by people who love to love, and people who love to do anything but.

It's wondering how much thinking about someone is too much thinking about someone. About learning how to give, and knowing when to stop. It's a foxy Slavoj Zizek making out platonically with a handsome Simone Weil in the middle of a university desperately trying to destroy its creative and liberal arts faculty. It's someone asking you "What do you need?", and for your only response to be "Can you hold me."
