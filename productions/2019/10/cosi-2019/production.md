# Cosi

## Date
17/10/2019 – 19/10/2019

## Year
2019

## Author
Louis Nowra

## Produced By
La Trobe University — FESTIVAL 2019 (Creative Arts Festival)

## Venue
Menzies Building, La Trobe University

## Location
Bundoora VIC, Australia

## Abstract
Student-led performance project as part of the La Trobe Creative Arts Festival. Lewis arrives to direct a play with inmates at a mental institution, persuaded by Roy to stage Mozart's Cosi Fan Tutte — in 1971 Melbourne, against the background of the Vietnam War.

## Director
Jessica Polites, Wiaan V Venter
