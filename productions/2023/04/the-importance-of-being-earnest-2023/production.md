# The Importance of Being Earnest

## Date
21/04/2023 – 22/04/2023

## Year
2023

## Author
Oscar Wilde

## Produced By
La Trobe Student Theatre and Film — Moat Arts Festival 2023

## Venue
Menzies Theatre, Menzies College DH Building

## Location
La Trobe University, Bundoora VIC, Australia

## Abstract
Two gentlemen have created alter egos to escape their responsibilities. Two ladies have created imaginary romances with men named Ernest. And Lady Bracknell has completely had it with all this creativity. The Importance of Being Earnest, a Trivial Play for Serious People, is a farcical comedy from the wit of Oscar Wilde.
